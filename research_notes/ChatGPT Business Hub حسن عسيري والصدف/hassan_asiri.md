# Hassan Asiri (حسن عسيري) — Saudi Public Figure Profile

## ⚠️ CRITICAL FLAG BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE

The research brief frames حسن عسيري / Hassan Asiri as a **singer/artist**. Every reliable source found describes him instead as a **Saudi actor, TV producer, and talk-show host** — specifically the founder/general manager of **"الصدف" (Al-Sadaf) للإنتاج الفني** (Sadaf for Audio and Visual Production), a major Saudi/Arab drama production company. No source found describes him releasing songs, albums, or having a music career. This appears to be a **mismatch between the assignment's premise and the real public figure**, not a gap in research. The report writer / client should confirm whether:
1. They mean this real person (producer, "Godfather of Saudi Drama"), and the "singer" framing in the brief is simply mistaken; or
2. They mean a different, same-named individual who is actually a singer (search surfaced a separate, very obscure "حسن محمد العسيري" credited as a composer/singer with at most one findable song — see Gaps below — who does not appear to be a significant public figure); or
3. The name/spelling is slightly off and they intend someone else entirely.

All findings below describe the **producer/actor/TV-host Hassan Asiri** (the only version of this name with a substantial, well-sourced public profile), since he is almost certainly the "حسن عسيري" the client means given the association with "الصدف للإنتاج الفني" named explicitly in the brief. Treat any "singer" framing used downstream as unverified and likely incorrect.

## What is his nationality, hometown, and career background (how he started, major breakthrough)?

### Takeaway
He is Saudi, born in February 1967 in Al-Kharj, Saudi Arabia; he started as an actor in a Jordanian series in 1988 while at university, later moved into production (including in Egypt from 1995), and built Sadaf into one of the largest private Arab TV production companies, partly owned/invested in by MBC Group since 2006.

### Cited Findings
- Born in Al-Kharj in February 1967; by 2010 he was managing production of more than 18 simultaneous series, per Al Jazirah — [Al Jazirah, Sept 2010](https://al-jazirah.com/2010/20100902/at4.htm)
- Birth date given as 23 February 1967 by manhom.com vs. 14/24 February 1967 by elCinema-sourced reporting — sources conflict on the exact day — [manhom.com](https://manhom.com/شخصيات/حسن-عسيري/); reported via [WebSearch summary of elCinema](https://elcinema.com/person/1102207/)
- First appeared as an actor in a Jordanian series in 1988 while a university student — [elCinema profile summary via search]
- He is a graduate of Imam Mohammed bin Saud Islamic University — [Al Rai Media interview, March 11 2026](https://www.alraimedia.com/article/1759346/فنون/منوعات/حسن-عسيري-المعاهد-الفنية-توازي-كليات-الطب-في-مخرجاتها)
- He has worked in Egyptian production since 1995 — [Al Rai Media interview](https://www.alraimedia.com/article/1759346/فنون/منوعات/حسن-عسيري-المعاهد-الفنية-توازي-كليات-الطب-في-مخرجاتها)
- He previously worked as a football players' agent ("سمسار" for football players) and has stated he does not believe a university degree is essential — [Sabq headline](https://sabq.org/article/-2KyJ3r) (full article text could not be fetched — 403 error; this is drawn from the search-result summary/headline only)

- Made a comeback as lead actor in the series "Ya Hali" (يا هلي) in Ramadan 2024 after an absence of more than seven years, airing on the Saudi channel and Shahid platform — [Sayidaty (via search summary)](https://www.sayidaty.net/node/1739271)
